Dashboard for the standalone server: a service selector (shown when more than one service has data; the choice is in the URL and sent as `service` to the request, error, route, class and map queries), a map that lays several services out with their calls to each other drawn apart from their calls to external targets, a "standalone server" badge with a note that statistics come from stored spans, a sign-out button, and a redirect to the login page on a 401.
